Location-Based Hydrology Analyser

Estimates hydrological parameters — AMDP, PMP, and climate variables — from real meteorological records, with LSTM-based flood forecasting and CNN weather image classification.

CNN Weather Image Analyser

Upload an outdoor sky / field weather photograph to classify weather conditions and get a hydrological risk assessment. The module extracts pixel-level features (brightness distribution, RGB channel stats, edge intensity, cloud coverage) and maps them to weather classes. Based on Maity, Srivastava, Sarkar & Khan (2024).
